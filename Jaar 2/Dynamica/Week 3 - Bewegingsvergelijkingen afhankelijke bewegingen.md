### Oefening 3

$v = 5 \pu{ m / s }$

$\sum{F_{y}} = 0$
$\sum{F_{y}} = F_{N} - mg$

$F_{N} = 10 \cdot 9.81 = 98.1$

$\sum{F_{x}} = m \cdot \ddot{x}$
$-98.1 \cdot 0.15 = 10 \cdot \ddot{x}$
$\ddot{x} = -1.47 \pu{ m / s }^2$

$\dot{x} = 5 - 1.47t$
$\dot{x} = 0$
