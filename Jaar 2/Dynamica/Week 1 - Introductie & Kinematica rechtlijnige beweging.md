## Oefening 1

$s = v \cdot t$

$v = 35 - \frac{25}{15}t$

Dus,

$s = \int_{0}^{15}[35 - \frac{25}{15}t]$

$s = 35 \cdot 15 - \frac{25}{15} \cdot 15^{2}$
$s = 337.5 \pu{m}$

## Oefening 2

$v(t) = 4t - 3t^{2}$

$s(t) = 2t^{2}-t^{3}+s(0)$

---

De positie op $t=4$
$s(4) = -32 \pu{m}$

---

De verplaatsing tussen $t=0$ en $t=4$

$4t-3t^{2}=0$
$t(4-3t)=0$
$t = 0$ en $t = 4/3$

$s = \int_{0}^{4/3}[4t-3t^{2]}+\int_{4/3}^{4}[4t-3t^2]$
